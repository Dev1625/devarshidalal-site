# Website voice

Write as Devarshi: a student thinking carefully about something he wants to understand. Use active first person for his work and judgments. Explain the idea in ordinary language, show how the evidence leads to the view, and leave open questions open.

Keep contractions, useful questions, parenthetical asides and uneven spoken rhythm when they fit. An occasional fragment can stay. Technical writing can be more restrained than an essay without turning into a different person. Labels, results tables and definitions do not need an “I” added to every line.

Avoid résumé fragments in personal descriptions, corporate abstractions, generic praise and endings that turn every observation into a lesson. Cut theatrical reveals, repeated “the real question” framing, empty intensifiers and stock contrasts. Prefer a specific reason over announcing that something is interesting. Do not add a joke, personal experience, certainty or doubt merely to make prose sound human. Preserve the author's argument and the details that make it his.

For healthcare research, distinguish source facts from my interpretation. Keep predictions dated and preserve reversals. Research coverage is not evidence of an actual investment or return. A lack of a public announcement is not proof that an event did not happen.

For Research, describe what I or the team did and what the analysis supports. Preserve sample sizes, numerical results, methods and limitations. A proposed therapeutic direction is still a hypothesis. Do not turn a workflow-quality model into a diagnostic claim.

For the Library, keep the exploratory argument and analogies. Attribute other people's ideas and preserve quotations. Avoid presenting a philosophical position as settled science. Start with the thought and stop when it is made; do not force every essay into the same opening, three-part structure or polished conclusion.

## Privacy and publication

The owner must approve the exact addition before new personal information from LifeOS, Drive or another private source is published on any page, including Home. Prepare the proposed wording privately and ask first. Do not commit private source records, drafts, links or review notes to the public repository. Approval of one addition does not approve other details about that project or the owner's life.

Existing public text can be edited for voice within the approved scope. Scheduled healthcare maintenance remains limited to Healthcare and related counts and metadata. It may publish healthcare updates directly, but it cannot add biography, personal experiences, projects, school or recruiting updates from private records.

## Review before publishing

Read the complete changed passage in context. Check that it still says what the original meant. Preserve quotes, source URLs, dates, figures, technical identifiers and historical predictions. Keep legitimate technical terms even if a pattern checker flags them, such as “pivotal trial,” “comprehensive stroke centre” or financial “leverage.”

Use Avoid AI Writing's pattern scan and preservation validator when available, followed by an editorial check for cadence, ownership and unsupported additions. Treat scores as writing signals. Do not distort a precise term or claim that a score proves human authorship. Run the appropriate site checks and inspect the published pages.
